SAVING LIVES THROUGH ALTERNATE OPTIONS (Z) LTD

Saving Lives Through Alternate Options (Z) Ltd was registered in 2006 but only became operational in 2018 due to funding challenges. Its focus and initial catchment area was Mwantalasha Village. This village is situated in the district of Chongwe, 45Km East of Lusaka (Zambia, Africa) along the Great East Road. The village has a population of approximately 4,500 most of whom are under the age of fifteen. It is uniquely\ situated and has attracted residents from urban areas who have settled there. Mwantalasha Village Education Project was conceived to address the educational crisis of both boy and girl children in the village. It began with only one child but has since grown to 104, some of whom have already graduated from the programme. We could not raise the funds locally and had to partner with a couple in Chester, United Kingdom by the name of Lorraine and David Cawley. They in turn shared the vision with like minded people in the United Kingdom and eventually formed a Charity registered within the United Kingdom as "MWANTALASHA VILLAGE EDUCATION PROJECT" with the sole purpose of raising funds for the project implementation in Zambia to ensure that all vulnerable children in the village have access to quality education.
