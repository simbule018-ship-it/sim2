COUNTRY OVERVIEW

Zambia is a landlocked country in Southern Africa, formerly colonized by Great Britain It gained independence on 24th October, 1964. It currently has a population of 24,000,000, the majority of whom are children. The country has had its own share of setbacks with HIV/AIDs and most recently, the Corona Virus. We have weathered the Corona Virus pandemic, but the effects of HIV/AIDs continue to plague the nation and it's people. It has had a lasting impact especially among children most of whom found themselves without parents from the disease. Coupled with these pandemics was the decline in the country's economy arising from instability in market forces that affected the mining industry which has been the back bone of our country. The situation saw massive losses of jobs, but with time, this has gradually been addressed. No doubt, it will take a long time to stabilize. The most affected by all these issues have been children and the girl child in particular. Loss of parents and reliable support systems left some children destitute and open to abuse and exploitation. The girl child in particular found themselves forced into early marriages which took them away from school and exposed them to serious reproductive complications.
