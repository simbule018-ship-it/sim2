PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The project is managed by the local board and volunteers. However, we have grown so much now with eighty-nine children that we are proposing to have a full time Project Coordinator responsible for the day to day administration of the organization. Every four months following our country's school calendar, we submit a comprehensive expense report to the Trustees of Mwantalasha Village Education Project Board of Trustees in the United Kingdom.

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

The Project is monitored on a regular basis with counselling services provided to both the children and their guardians.
