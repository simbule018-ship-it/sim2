Background

The Mwantalasha Vilage Education project is a charity organization dedicated to providing Educational support to children in Mwantalasha village, Zambia. the project`s primary objective is to enable these children to complete their Education and improve their wellbeing

MISSION

The project aims to break the cycle of poverty by providing funding to saving lives through alternate options (Z) Limited (SLAO), which supports the Education and wellbeing of children in the village.

IMPACT

The project has already Shown positive results, with some sponsored children going on to universites and others qualifying as professionals, such as nusrsing assistants and HGV/PSV drivers For instance, Justina Tembo, a sponsored Child, was trained as a Nursing assistant and is now employed as a Home Care Assistant.
